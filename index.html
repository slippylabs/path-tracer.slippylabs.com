PATH TRACER

A little rendering engine in a background thread. It fires rays at a scene, bounces them around until they find light, and averages the result — the image sharpens as the samples pile up.

All the tracing happens on a Web Worker, so the page stays responsive while it grinds. Noise falls off with the square root of the sample count — four times the samples for half the grain, which is why the last bit of cleanup always takes the longest. The furnace test is the honest one: a perfectly white diffuse sphere sitting in a uniformly white environment must become completely invisible, because a surface that reflects all the light it receives cannot be any darker than its surroundings. If the sphere is still visible against the background, the maths is losing or inventing energy, and the error figure above says by how much.
